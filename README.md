## 🌟 Overview

**PlutoTax Consulting & Advisory LLC** is a premium corporate tax consulting and financial advisory web application. It is engineered specifically for US citizens, Green Card holders, H-1B / L-1 / F-1 OPT visa professionals, startups, small businesses, and Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) requiring sophisticated US and international cross-border tax solutions.

## 🚀 Key Functional Sections & Features

1. **Top Contact Bar & Sticky Header**
   - Headquartered in Austin, TX with direct phone, email, and social channels.
   - Original PlutoTax logo embedded seamlessly with zero patchwork.
   - Sticky navbar with drop-shadow on scroll and responsive mobile drawer.

2. **Hero Section (Desktop & Mobile Specialized Composition)**
   - **Desktop Background:** High-resolution US Capitol architectural backdrop with animated lighting effects and golden curves.
   - **Mobile Background:** Stacked, mobile-optimized visual composition with bright contrast and zero horizontal overflow.
   - Headline: *"Smart Tax Solutions. Greater Financial Confidence."*

3. **Statistics Strip (Immediately Below Hero)**
   - Deep navy background with subtle dotted matrix texture.
   - Animated counter display:
     - `15,000+` Tax Returns Filed
     - `$12M+` Refunds Maximized
     - `99.8%` Client Satisfaction
   - Verification control managed via the Admin Console.

4. **Dedicated Client Portal Section (Directly Below Statistics Strip)**
   - *"Your Tax Journey, All in One Place."*
   - Interactive document vault, filing progress milestones, and CPA messaging.

5. **Our Professional Services (9 Dedicated Specializations)**
   - Advance Tax Planning & Tax Strategy (`/services/tax-planning`)
   - Federal & Multi-State Tax Preparation (1040 / 1040NR) (`/services/federal-tax-preparation`)
   - FBAR & FATCA Compliance (`/services/fbar-fatca`)
   - ITIN Processing (Certifying Acceptance Agent – CAA) (`/services/itin-processing`)
   - Business Tax Filing & Bookkeeping (`/services/business-tax`)
   - Indian Cross-Border Tax Filing (NRI Solutions) (`/services/nri-tax-solutions`)
   - Tax Extension Filing (Form 4868 / 7004) (`/services/tax-extension`)
   - Audit Assistance & IRS Notice Representation (`/services/audit-assistance`)
   - Year-Round Advisory & Wealth Tax Consulting (`/services/wealth-advisory`)
   - Each service has its own dedicated page with deep explanations, checklists, interactive FAQ accordions, and consultation CTAs.

6. **Interactive 4-Step Process & Modal Popups**
   - Step 01: Register & Upload Documents
   - Step 02: CPA Review & Tax Calculation
   - Step 03: Review & Confirmation
   - Step 04: IRS E-Filing & Status Tracking
   - Clicking any step launches a modal popup with detailed procedures, timelines, and next/previous navigation.

7. **Quick Refund Estimator**
   - Interactive 2024/2025 federal tax bracket calculator supporting Single, Married Filing Jointly, Married Filing Separately, and Head of Household.
   - Accommodates resident and non-resident 1040-NR parameters.
   - Clear disclaimers and consultation triggers.

8. **What Our Clients Say (Verified Reviews Carousel)**
   - Carousel with automated progression, pause-on-hover, avatar previews, and star ratings.

9. **Consultation & Intake Portal**
   - Real-time client form submission with field validation, preferred contact selection, and backend persistence into the Admin Console.

10. **Administrative Management Console (`/admin`)**
    - Secure passcode-gated console (`pluto2025`).
    - Validate and verify performance statistics.
    - Review and manage inbound consultation inquiries.
    - Toggle testimonial publication status.
    - Edit live service descriptions dynamically.

11. **Regulatory & Security Pages**
    - Official Privacy Policy with IRC § 7216 non-disclosure declaration.
    - Terms of Service & Circular 230 standards.
    - Bank-grade AES-256 data security overview.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Framework:** React 18 + Vite 6 + TypeScript 5
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS + PostCSS + Autoprefixer
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Animations:** CSS3 Keyframes & Smooth Hardware Accelerated Transforms
- **Routing:** React Router DOM v6
